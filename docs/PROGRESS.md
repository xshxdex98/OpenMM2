Progress is measured in **bytes of machine code**, not function count. A class of
eighty one-line accessors is an afternoon; `sdlPage16` is nineteen functions and
47 KB of city tessellator. Counting functions would rank the first four times higher.

Two numbers matter and they are different:

- **Ported** - actually reimplemented. An `ARTS_EXPORT` declaration whose `PROC` has
  been stripped from `game.asm`. This is the real number.
- **Ready** - could be ported now without guessing. This needs the MEMBER layout,
  not just the size: the remaining assembly reads fields at fixed offsets, so they
  must be declared. A size on its own only pins `check_size`.

## Shape of the work

- The **20 largest classes are 39% of all code**. The port is front-loaded: a
  small number of large classes dominate, and the long tail is cheap.
- **Median function is 64 bytes** - most of the binary is small functions.
- **1938 virtual methods across 340 vftables.** Constructors must be ported together
  with every virtual of their class, or the vtable the compiler emits will not link.
- Largest class not yet ported: **`aiVehiclePhysics`** at 58.7 KB.
- **290 classes (591.2 KB, 34% of code) have no known layout** and are blocked: their
  member offsets would have to be guessed, which is how you get silent corruption.
